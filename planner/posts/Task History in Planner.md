## Task history - the where's waldo of Planner

Here's where you can access the history of changes on a Task:

1. Click the "`i`" details icon in the far right of the `Task Name` column
2. 1. The Task details are shown to the right
3. Click the small clock button in the top right of the task details

![[MS Planner - Task Details - History.png]]

Once you click the clock button the Task pane shifts to the left and the "Changes" pane appears: it's magic and `+1` Microsoft.

![[MS Planner - Task Changes.png]]

Task - Changes pane

If you click another task on the left, Planner keeps the change history visible and shows the history for the task you selected. Nice job Microsoft `+1`.

Zooming in on the changes section, you see who made the change, roughly when ("Several days ago"), what they changed. In this example Gary changed the custom field I have called `Area` and its value to `Kitchen`. A month ago Gary added the label `KK1.`

![[MS Planner - Changes.png]]

That's a pretty handy feature overall ... but ... the issue is that it doesn't show the actual date and time that the change was made, so that's a `-1` Microsoft.
